## 2. The question

## 3. The explanation

- **Plain-English meaning:** What you *do* proves more than what you *say* —
  promises and claims mean little without follow-through.
- **Origin (1 line):** Traced to a similar Latin phrase and popularized in
  English by the 1600s; the modern wording is credited to Abraham Lincoln
  in some (disputed) attributions.
- **Example sentence:** "He kept saying he'd change, but actions speak
  louder than words — and he never did."

VO/caption script:
> If someone keeps promising but never follows through, this is the
> phrase for it. What people *do* tells you more than what they *say*.
